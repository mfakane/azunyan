title: Privacy Policy
description: Azunote collects no information about you, and reads and writes only the files you work with and its own folders.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Azunote does not collect, send, or share any information about you or how
you use it. It has no telemetry, analytics, advertising, or accounts, and it
does not connect to the internet on its own.

## Files Azunote reads and writes

Azunote reads and writes only:

- The files you open or save, and those you go to through its features,
  such as Open Recent or Go to Definition.
- Its settings folder, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Azunote`, or the `appdata` folder of
  the portable version. It holds your settings, language modes, external
  tools, the list of recent files, and copies of unsaved documents kept for
  the next launch. See [Settings](/azunyan/docs/settings/).
- `%TEMP%\Azunote`, where error logs and crash dumps are written when
  something goes wrong, and the temporary folder, where copies of a document
  are briefly kept while an external tool reads them. These stay on your PC.

## Programs you set up

[External tools](/azunyan/docs/external-tools/) and
[language servers](/azunyan/docs/language-servers/) are programs you choose
for Azunote to run. What they read, write, or send is up to those programs.

## Microsoft Store

When you install Azunote from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the
download and updates under the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
